Amarillo Gearbox Repair & Model Identification

Amarillo Gear builds distinct fan-drive, pump-drive, and industrial gearbox families. Start with the series and complete model number, then record ratio, shaft arrangement, mounting, and operating conditions before evaluating repair or replacement.

An Amarillo brand name alone does not identify the unit. Its official datasheets group fan drives, A-Series units, and pump drives separately; the right drawing and parts references depend on that family and model.

Separate Amarillo fan drives from pump drives

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Fan-drive modelsThe datasheet library separates single- and double-reduction models, including 65A through 175 and double-reduction designations such as 1008 and 1110.Copy the complete model, ratio, input/output speeds, mounting orientation, and plate serial/order data.
A-Series drivesA-Series designations include single- and double-reduction configurations; do not treat them as interchangeable with a fan-drive model.Photograph the plate and housing, then record shaft directions and connected equipment.
Pump drivesRight-angle pump-drive products have a different application and shaft arrangement from tower-fan units.Record pump type, engine or motor input, vertical/horizontal orientation, and coupling details.

What to record before opening the unit

Log oil level and condition, operating temperature, load changes, vibration, recent alignment or foundation work, and any water or debris exposure. These observations help frame inspection; they do not identify a failed component by themselves.

Brand and product names identify equipment only. They do not imply manufacturer affiliation or authorization. Service suitability, parts sourcing, timing, and scope must be confirmed for the exact unit before shipment or work authorization.

Plan an equipment-specific inquiry

Prepare the gearbox nameplate, full-unit photos, machine or application details, operating symptoms, load and speed history, lubricant records, and available drawings. The repair brief helps organize those details for a review.

Amarillo Gear gearbox questions

How do I identify an Amarillo gearbox?

Record the full model and serial or order information from the nameplate, then distinguish fan-drive, A-Series, or pump-drive configuration using the matching manufacturer datasheet.

Are Amarillo fan-drive and pump-drive units interchangeable?

No interchange should be assumed from the brand name. Confirm model, ratio, shaft arrangement, mounting, and application against the correct drawing.

What should I send for an Amarillo gearbox review?

Send clear photos of every plate and the whole unit, plus application, symptoms, operating history, ratio, mounting position, and any prior repair records.
