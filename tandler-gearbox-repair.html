Tandler Gearbox Repair & Identification

Tandler makes precision spiral-bevel, servo, and speed-modulation gearboxes. A speed-modulation unit uses an additional shaft to combine or adjust drive speed, so record the full shaft arrangement and its motion-control role.

Tandler's product range separates spiral-bevel gearboxes, servo gearboxes, speed-modulation units, high-speed/high-torque products, and custom designs. Identify the family before using a catalog or comparing another unit.

Distinguish a Tandler speed modulator from a standard reducer

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Speed-modulation gearboxA third shaft can superimpose speed or phase in a synchronized process.Record all input/output shafts, shaft direction, gear ratio, control or adjustment method, and process timing.
Spiral-bevel gearboxRight-angle transmission is available with multiple shaft and mounting arrangements.Photograph the housing, shaft orientation, mounting, ratio plate, and connected machine.
Servo gearboxTandler's servo portfolio includes combinations of planetary and bevel gearing for dynamic motion applications.Include motor model, positioning/backlash needs, output interface, duty cycle, and any motion-control alarms.

Capture the motion requirement, not just the power rating

For synchronized equipment, document the required relative speed or phase and the machine's operating sequence. Record vibration, temperature, noise, backlash observations, and recent control or coupling changes separately.

Tandler gearbox questions

What does a Tandler speed-modulation gearbox do?

Tandler describes speed-modulation gearboxes as adding a third shaft to superimpose speed or phase for synchronized production and processing.

Is every Tandler gearbox a servo reducer?

No. The product range includes spiral-bevel, servo, speed-modulation, high-speed/high-torque, and custom gearboxes.

What information is important for identifying a speed modulator?

Record every shaft's role and orientation, unit series, ratio, motion/phase requirements, connected drive, and machine operating history.
