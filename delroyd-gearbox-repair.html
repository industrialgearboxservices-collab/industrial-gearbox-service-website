Delroyd Worm Gearbox Repair & Identification

Delroyd's single-worm reducers use right-angle gearing in horizontal or vertical arrangements. The maker's plate contains model and operating information that should be captured before selecting parts or a service path.

Delroyd documentation identifies catalog number, shop order number, service horsepower, ratio, service factor, input/output RPM, and figure number as useful plate fields. The shop order can connect a unit with its original bill of materials.

Use the Delroyd plate to identify the reducer

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Catalog and shop-order numberThe catalog number describes the unit model; Delroyd says the shop-order number defines its original bill of materials.Photograph the entire plate and transcribe every legible field, including faded or stamped marks.
Ratio and operating ratingsInput/output RPM, service horsepower, and service factor help match the reducer to its application.Record present motor speed, measured output speed, load type, and operating duration.
Worm and output arrangementDelroyd products include different shaft and mounting arrangements; the catalog selection must match the installed configuration.Photograph shaft extensions, mounting feet/flanges, motor adapter, and any cooling accessories.

Record lubrication and heat observations

Document lubricant type and service history from the equipment records, leakage location, running temperature, and whether the reducer operates continuously or intermittently. Use the exact applicable manual for any service procedure.

Delroyd Worm Gear gearbox questions

Where can I find Delroyd gearbox identification data?

Check the unit nameplate for catalog number, shop-order number, ratio, service horsepower, input/output RPM, service factor, and figure number.

What should I photograph on a Delroyd reducer?

Capture the whole nameplate, the reducer from multiple sides, input/output shaft arrangement, mounting, motor adapter, and any cooling or lubrication equipment.

Can a Delroyd worm set replace another brand's gears?

Do not assume interchange from brand or center distance alone. Confirm tooth geometry, ratio, shaft arrangement, fit, material, and the actual reducer design.
