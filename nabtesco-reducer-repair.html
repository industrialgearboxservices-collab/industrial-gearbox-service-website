Nabtesco Precision Reducer Identification & Repair

Direct answer: Nabtesco’s RV precision reduction gears are used in motion-control assemblies and are offered as components and gearheads. Identify the exact RV or gearhead series, model, shaft style and motor interface before seeking service.

Start with the series name and complete type code

Nabtesco’s RV products are precision reducers, not general-purpose industrial speed reducers. Its lineup separates basic reducer components from gearheads prepared for motor mounting; the families also vary by shaft style and input arrangement. This distinction matters for repair planning, especially when the reducer is built into a servo or robotics assembly.

The precision gear portfolio includes RV component reducers and assembled gearheads such as RD2, RS, RH and GH families.

Identify an RV precision reduction gear or assembled gearhead

Before quoting or shipping, establish whether the part is a standalone RV component or an assembled gearhead. Record servo-motor make/model, adapter or flange, input direction, output shaft type, backlash specification if known, and any motion-control symptoms. Because precision requirements are application-specific, service applicability for the exact unit needs confirmation before work is authorized.

Nabtesco RV precision reducer questions

What Nabtesco reducer information should I record?

Capture the RV or gearhead family, complete model, ratio, shaft type, motor interface, and the equipment where it is installed.

What is the difference between a Nabtesco component and a gearhead?

Nabtesco describes the component as a basic precision gearbox integrated by the customer; a gearhead is an assembled unit with motor-mounting provisions.

Are RV-C and RV-N the same shaft configuration?

No. The product guide identifies RV-N as a solid-shaft family and RV-C as a hollow-shaft family. Verify the exact model against current documentation.

Can any gearbox shop service a Nabtesco precision reducer?

Do not assume so. Confirm the exact model, precision requirements, and service capability before authorizing work or shipment.

What symptoms should I include in an inquiry?

Describe positioning error, backlash change, noise, heat, vibration, load, duty cycle, and any measurement method or alarm history.
