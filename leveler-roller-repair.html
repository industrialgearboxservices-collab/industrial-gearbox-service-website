Leveler Roller Repair

What does this roller do in the process?

A roller leveler uses a configured series of rolls to bend strip or plate through alternating passes. Work rolls contact the material, while backup rolls support the leveling arrangement; roll positions and cassette details matter to identify the part. Identify the complete machine and the roll’s position within it; names and requirements vary among manufacturers and applications.

What should I document before requesting roller repair?

  • Identify the leveler manufacturer, machine model, cassette, and the specific roll position.
  • Record roll face and journal dimensions, material grade, thickness range, and available setup records.
  • Document surface marks or measured runout and relate them to the material path and line.

What details help scope leveler roller repair?

Include the line and roller manufacturer, model or part number, face and journal dimensions, surface or covering details, operating material, and symptom history. Add drawings, photos, and maintenance records where available. Share your required date and facility location; confirm scope, schedule, and receiving arrangements before shipment.

Technical context

Roller leveling process describes leveling through a series of rollers; use the specific machine documentation for roll positions and settings.

Leveler Roller Repair FAQs

Why does the roll position matter on a leveler?

Work and backup rolls have different positions and functions in the cassette. Their dimensions, contact conditions, and support arrangement are not interchangeable, so identify the cassette and roll location from the machine drawing.

What details help scope a leveler roll repair?

Provide the leveler make and model, cassette and roll position, dimensions, material being processed, relevant setup records, and observed surface or dimensional issue. Include drawings and inspection measurements if available.
