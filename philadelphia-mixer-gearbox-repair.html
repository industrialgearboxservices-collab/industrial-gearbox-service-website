Philadelphia Mixer Gearbox Repair Planning

Philadelphia Mixing Solutions mixer gearboxes are separate from Philadelphia Gear industrial gearboxes. Confirm which company and product are on the equipment plate before using the right repair-planning information.

Manufacturer names and product references on this page are for equipment identification. They do not imply manufacturer affiliation, authorization, or guaranteed parts availability. Confirm service suitability and scope for the exact unit before shipment or work authorization.

Philadelphia Mixing Solutions is distinct from Philadelphia Gear

This guide covers gear-driven mixers and their reducer assemblies from Philadelphia Mixing Solutions. It is not a substitute for the separate Philadelphia Gear gearbox guide. Similar names do not establish common product design, parts, or service lineage.

RAVEN example: identify the exact size and ratio

The Philadelphia Mixing Solutions parts catalog documents a RAVEN gear-driven top-entry mixer, including a Size 3853 example with double-reduction ratios from 6.2:1 through 31.4:1. The catalog organizes parts by ratio and component, showing why the mixer series, size, and exact ratio are needed. These data describe that catalog example, not every Philadelphia mixer gearbox.

Capture mixer model and serial, gearbox size and nameplate, reduction ratio, number and orientation of output shafts, motor mounting, coupling arrangement, and available assembly or parts drawings. Record whether the equipment is top-entry, side-entry, or another arrangement.

Inspection planning for a mixer drive

Document mixer duty, batch cycle, starts and stops, process material, load changes, oil and lubrication history, and symptoms such as new noise, vibration, heat, leakage, or output-shaft movement. Note whether the symptom appears during startup, mixing, discharge, or a particular process stage. These observations guide inspection but do not establish a failure cause.

How can mixer size and ratio identify a Philadelphia mixer drive?

Record mixer and drive plates, model, size and ratio, serial/order number, sectional drawing or bill of materials, shaft orientation, and whether the observation concerns the reducer, motor, coupling, mounting, or mixer shaft. References: parts portal and product parts catalogue.

Philadelphia Mixer Gearbox questions

Is Philadelphia Mixing Solutions the same as Philadelphia Gear?

They are distinct names and product contexts. Check the equipment plate and product documentation; use the Philadelphia Gear guide for Philadelphia Gear industrial gearboxes.

What ratios are listed for the RAVEN Size 3853 example?

The Philadelphia Mixing Solutions catalog lists a RAVEN double-reduction Size 3853 example with ratios from 6.2:1 to 31.4:1. Verify the installed mixer size and ratio.

What details help identify a Philadelphia mixer gearbox?

Provide the mixer and gearbox plates, model and serial, size, ratio, output arrangement, motor and coupling details, drawings, and operating history.
