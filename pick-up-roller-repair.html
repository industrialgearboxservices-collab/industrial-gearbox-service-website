Pick-Up Roller Repair

In papermaking, a suction pick-up roll transfers the sheet from a forming section to a felt using vacuum through the roll shell. The suction zone, seals, shell, and felt interface are part of the operating arrangement.

Prepare a repair brief ↗

What should I document before requesting roller repair?

  • Identify the paper machine, pick-up roll position, shell dimensions, and suction-zone configuration.
  • Record vacuum readings and operating history from plant records, including felt and seal observations.
  • Map sheet transfer symptoms and note whether they vary across the machine width.

Before shipment, confirm the agreed service scope, schedule, and receiving arrangements.

Technical context

Roll suction-zone technical paper discusses suction-zone and roll modifications; it is not a repair instruction for a specific machine.

What does a paper-machine suction pick-up roll do?

In a paper machine, a suction pick-up roll can help transfer the forming web onto a felt using vacuum through a perforated shell. The vacuum zone, seals, felt, and web path are part of the transfer arrangement, so identify the complete position rather than describing the component only as an idler.

Valmet’s suction-roll modernization paper discusses suction rolls, zones, and seals. It supplies process context, not a diagnosis or universal repair procedure.

  • Identify the paper machine section, pick-up-roll position, shell dimensions, and suction-zone layout from records.
  • Record available vacuum readings and whether they vary across the machine width.
  • Note felt and seal inspection history documented by the plant.
  • Describe web-transfer issues, edge behavior, or breaks and when they occur.

A transfer problem may involve the roll, vacuum system, felt, seals, or process conditions. The observations help scope the review without assigning root cause.

Pick-Up Roller Repair FAQs

What should I document if sheet transfer at the pick-up roll is unstable?

Record the machine and roll identification, vacuum readings, suction-zone details, felt condition, and where transfer changes occur across the width. Include recent maintenance and operating history to help evaluate the entire transfer arrangement.

Does low vacuum prove the pick-up roll needs repair?

No. Vacuum performance can involve the roll, seals, suction system, piping, and operating setup. Record measured values and recent changes before attributing the issue to a single component.
