GEARBOX IDENTIFICATION & REPAIR PLANNING

Konecranes Gearbox Repair Planning & Identification

A Konecranes hoist gearbox is part of a lifting mechanism, not a generic reducer installation. Identify the hoist family and mechanism, then record the gearbox plate and crane details before service planning.

Konecranes C-Series materials describe a purpose-built motor, controls, and gearbox as integrated hoist components. Other crane drives may serve travel, hoisting, or slewing functions; keep the mechanism and hoist model attached to the gearbox record.

Identify the hoist mechanism before the gearbox

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
C-Series electric chain hoistThe gearbox is documented as one element of the C-Series hoist drive assembly.Record hoist model, capacity, serial or production data, gearbox plate, motor/control identifiers, and hoisting duty.
Crane travel or slewing driveTravel and slewing gearboxes serve different mechanisms and have different installation interfaces.Record crane model, mechanism location, gear ratio, motor/brake details, and connected wheel or slew structure.
Legacy hoist or gearboxOlder units may have incomplete plates or modified assemblies.Photograph all labels and cast markings; include crane serial, year, mechanism, drawings, and prior maintenance records.

Safety and service boundary

This page is for identification and repair planning. Follow the applicable crane/hoist documentation and site safety procedures; do not use this guide as a lifting or disassembly procedure. Record the exact symptom and operating circumstance.

Prepare the gearbox nameplate, full-unit photos, machine or application details, operating symptoms, load and speed history, lubricant records, and available drawings. The repair brief helps organize those details for a review.

Konecranes gearbox questions

Is a C-Series hoist gearbox a standard reducer?

Konecranes describes the C-Series gearbox as part of an integrated hoist component set with its motor and controls; identify the assembly as a hoist.

Which gearbox plate details matter on a Konecranes unit?

Capture the gearbox type code, ratio, serial/job data if shown, and the crane/hoist mechanism it serves.

Can a gearbox be replaced without checking hoist ratings?

No. Verify the hoist and crane design, lifting duty, ratio, motor/brake/control interfaces, and applicable manufacturer documentation.
