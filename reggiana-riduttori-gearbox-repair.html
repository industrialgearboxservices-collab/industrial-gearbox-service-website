Reggiana Riduttori Gearbox Repair & Identification

Reggiana Riduttori offers planetary, helical, wheel, winch, and combined gearbox families. Identify the product series and complete unit configuration before applying a catalog or repair decision.

The official download area separates 2000 Series and PLUS planetary catalogs from helical, W Series winch, and combined V Series documentation. A family match narrows the right technical reference; it does not establish interchangeability.

Check stages, orientation, and installed interfaces

Before service planning, document the gearbox's mounting position, shaft type, connection to motor or machine, lubrication arrangement, and any brake or wheel-drive components attached to the assembly.

Reggiana Riduttori gearbox questions

How do I tell a 2000 Series unit from a PLUS unit?

Use the full type plate and series-specific product manual. The manufacturer publishes separate catalog and technical-manual families.

Does every Reggiana Riduttori gearbox use planetary gearing?

No. The official product range also lists helical, winch, wheel, and combined gearbox families.

Which details help identify a Reggiana gearbox?

Provide the full plate, series, ratio, stages, output and mounting configuration, and photos showing connected accessories and the application.
