Foote-Jones Gearbox & Speed Reducer Planning

Identify a Foote-Jones gearbox or speed reducer and organize the records needed for parts, repair, or replacement evaluation.

Foote-Jones gearbox and Foote Jones speed reducer inquiries should retain the complete original designation. Search spelling may vary, but a service request needs the precise equipment information rather than an approximate brand-and-model match.

Preserve the full nameplate record

Photograph the plate clearly and transcribe all readable identifiers. If a marking resembles “8255 SL,” retain the photograph and exact spacing or suffixes for verification. That search phrase alone does not establish dimensions, ratio, capacity, or parts compatibility.

Connect legacy records with the present unit

Regal Rexnord publishes a Foote-Jones gearbox-parts resource. Use manufacturer information as a starting point and compare it with the installed equipment and its repair history. Record previous component substitutions and distinguish an original drawing from a later revision.

Organize the repair decision

  • List the operating symptoms and the machine driven by the reducer.
  • Identify available drawings, parts lists, and prior repair reports.
  • Ask which components require inspection before a parts decision is possible.
  • Define the interfaces that any replacement must preserve.

When identification is incomplete

Supply overall photographs, visible casting or component markings, and available machine records. Treat a preliminary identification as provisional until checked. Avoid ordering a component from appearance alone or assuming that a similarly named reducer shares internal parts. A useful proposal makes identification uncertainties explicit and separates investigation from approved repair work.

Manufacturer reference

Regal Rexnord: Foote-Jones gearbox parts. This manufacturer resource supports equipment identification and planning; it does not establish Industrial Gearbox Service as an authorized repair center or parts distributor.

Prepare your equipment details

Record the nameplate, application, symptoms, facility location, and required date in the repair brief. The current private draft saves the brief to your device; it does not send a request. Service suitability, parts availability, and delivery arrangements require confirmation.
