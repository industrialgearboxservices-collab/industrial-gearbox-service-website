GEARBOX IDENTIFICATION & REPAIR PLANNING

Allen Gears Gearbox Repair Planning

Allen Gears units require identification from the gearbox itself and its service history. Baker Hughes groups Allen Gears and Lufkin under its gear-products portfolio, but the brand name alone does not establish the unit’s design, parts path, or interchangeability.

Manufacturer names and product references on this page are for equipment identification. They do not imply manufacturer affiliation, authorization, or guaranteed parts availability. Confirm service suitability and scope for the exact unit before shipment or work authorization.

Separate Allen Gears from Lufkin identification

Allen Gears and Lufkin appear together in Baker Hughes product information, but an Allen unit should not be identified as a Lufkin gearbox without a matching nameplate, drawing, or documented lineage. Record the exact maker wording, model or type, serial or shop number, ratio, speeds, rotation, and any drawing number.

What product information can and cannot tell you

Baker Hughes describes engineered gearbox and gear-coupling products that include low-speed and high-speed applications. This broad portfolio context is not a substitute for the individual Allen gearbox drawing. Preserve coupling details, shaft heights, mounting dimensions, lubrication-system information, and the driven machine’s duty data.

For an older or modified unit, compare the plate with historical purchase records, inspection reports, and prior rebuild notes. Do not assume a current product family or a replacement listed under another portfolio name is a direct fit.

Repair review versus replacement review

  • Repair review: clarify whether the scope includes the gearcase, couplings, lube system, base, and alignment checks.
  • Replacement review: confirm torque and speed, shaft interfaces, mounting, rotation, service factor, and connected equipment before comparing a substitute.
  • Before shipment: request confirmation of scope, parts sourcing, and service suitability for the exact unit.

Official product reference

Baker Hughes: Lufkin Gears and Allen Gears provides portfolio context. Use an equipment-specific drawing or nameplate to confirm the actual configuration.

Allen Gears Gearbox questions

How do I identify an Allen Gears gearbox?

Photograph the complete gearbox nameplate and record the model or type, serial or shop number, ratio, speeds, rotation, and any drawing number. Include the driven machine and gearbox arrangement.

Are Allen Gears and Lufkin gearboxes interchangeable?

The portfolio association does not establish interchangeability. Confirm the exact model, drawing, dimensions, rating, and interfaces for the individual gearbox.

What records help plan an Allen Gears repair?

Provide gearbox and motor plates, coupling and mounting photos, drawings, lubricant records, operating history, and prior inspection or rebuild documents.
