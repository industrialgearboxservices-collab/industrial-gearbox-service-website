Zambello Extruder Gearbox Repair & Identification

Zambello designs gearboxes for extrusion equipment, including single-screw and twin-screw configurations. Identify the extruder type and gearbox series before comparing repair and replacement options.

Zambello's product pages separate single-screw extruder reducers from co-rotating and counter-rotating twin-screw gearbox families. The single-screw range includes sizes 90 through 630; use the unit's own plate and drawing rather than inferring size from the extruder barrel.

Distinguish Zambello single-screw and twin-screw drives

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Single-screw extruder gearboxThe maker lists a dedicated size range and series for single-screw applications.Record gearbox size/series, ratio, screw speed, motor input, output shaft and thrust-bearing arrangement.

Keep gearbox symptoms separate from extrusion symptoms

Record when the sound, heat, oil leakage, or vibration occurs: startup, steady production, speed change, material change, or shutdown. Also note output behavior and any process or bearing alarms.

Zambello gearbox questions

What makes a Zambello gearbox different from a general reducer?

Zambello's cited product range is organized around extrusion equipment and screw configuration, so machine and gearbox identification should be considered together.

How do I identify a single-screw Zambello unit?

Use its gearbox plate and matching series documentation. The manufacturer lists single-screw reducers in sizes 90 to 630, but the size must be confirmed on the unit.

What should I send about a twin-screw gearbox?

Send gearbox and extruder plates, photos of both outputs, screw configuration, ratio/speed information, process history, and available drawings.
