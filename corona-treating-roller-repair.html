SHAFT & ROLLER SERVICE

Corona Treating Roller Repair

In a corona-treatment station, a treater roll may serve as a counter-electrode beneath a high-voltage electrode. The roll covering, electrode geometry, generator, and electrical configuration must be considered together; use the equipment maker’s limits for the specific station.

Prepare a repair brief ↗

What should I document before requesting roller repair?

  • Record treater and generator make and model, electrode type, and roll position.
  • Identify the roll covering or dielectric material from records; do not infer it by appearance alone.
  • Document arcing, contamination, surface marks, and when the issue occurs without opening energized equipment.

Before shipment, confirm the agreed service scope, schedule, and receiving arrangements.

Technical context

Corona treater station design describes system-specific treater construction; follow the equipment maker’s electrical safety procedures.

Corona Treating Roller Repair FAQs

Can I change a treater roll covering based only on a surface mark?

No. The covering and electrical characteristics must match the station design and equipment requirements. Record the roll identification and consult the treater documentation before specifying a material or replacement.

What should I include in a corona treater roll inquiry?

Provide the treater and generator model, roll dimensions, covering details from records, electrode arrangement, observed surface condition, and any relevant maintenance history. Do not inspect energized components; use site safety procedures.
