Liebherr Gearbox Repair Planning & Identification

Liebherr's gearbox portfolio includes slewing, swivelling, travel, planetary plug-in, splitter, and customized gearboxes. Identify the product family and machine interface before defining an inspection scope.

Liebherr publishes multiple gearbox types for different equipment. A travel drive, slewing drive, planetary plug-in gearbox, and splitter box have different interfaces and should not share an assumed repair specification.

Identify which Liebherr gearbox family is installed

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Travel or slewing driveThese gearbox families are used in distinct motion functions and machine assemblies.Record the machine model, drive location, gearbox type plate, ratio, input/output interface, and brake or motor details.
Planetary plug-in gearboxThe plug-in configuration makes the connection to the surrounding drive assembly part of identification.Photograph the fitted assembly, motor/adapter interface, output connection, and any coupling or seal arrangement.
Splitter or customized gearboxA special gearbox may be application-specific rather than a catalog interchange.Find the machine serial number, drawing or order reference, exact plate data, and operating history.

Define the equipment boundary before evaluating condition

Clarify whether the inquiry concerns the gearbox alone or a larger drive assembly. Include adjacent motor, brake, hydraulic/electrical connections, mounting structure, and any related alarms or movement issues.

Liebherr gearbox questions

Does Liebherr use the same gearbox design across all machines?

No. Liebherr's official portfolio lists several gearbox families and customized solutions. Identify the machine and drive function before comparing parts or units.

How do I identify a Liebherr travel or slewing gearbox?

Send the gearbox plate and machine serial/model, plus photos showing its mounting location and connected motor, brake, and output components.

Can I select a replacement by dimensions alone?

Dimensions alone are not enough. Confirm duty, ratio, torque, output interfaces, mounting, and any machine-control or brake requirements.
