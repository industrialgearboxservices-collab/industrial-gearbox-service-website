Gearbox Brand Guides & Repair Identification

Find brand-specific gearbox repair planning, nameplate identification, rebuild questions, and replacement-parts guidance.

Use these equipment guides to prepare a clearer gearbox repair, gear drive service, or replacement-parts inquiry. Each guide addresses a different identification or planning problem. Brand names identify equipment; they do not imply manufacturer affiliation, authorization, or confirmed service availability.

Brand-specific planning guides

  • NORD Gearbox Repair & Replacement Parts — Identify a NORD gearbox, plan a rebuild, and prepare a replacement-parts inquiry using the unit’s nameplate and application details.
  • Falk Gearbox & Gear Reducer Repair — Plan a Falk gear reducer repair around the unit identification, connected equipment, and a written inspection and rebuild scope.
  • Flender Gearbox Repair Planning — Prepare a Flender repair inquiry with a condition history, defined inspection scope, and clear return-to-service requirements.
  • SEW-EURODRIVE Repair & Parts Planning — Prepare a SEW-EURODRIVE gearbox or geared-motor repair request using serial-number identification and a clear mechanical and electrical scope.
  • Cone Drive Gearbox Repair & Reconditioning — Plan Cone Drive repair, gearset evaluation, and parts inquiries around the identified assembly and application requirements.
  • Lufkin Gearbox Repair & Service Records — Prepare a Lufkin gear repair evaluation with equipment identification, operating history, and defined inspection and acceptance requirements.
  • Dodge Industrial Gearbox Repair & Replacement — Identify an industrial Dodge reducer and compare repair or replacement against its configuration, mounting, and driven-equipment needs.
  • Marley Gearbox & Geareducer Repair — Plan Marley cooling-tower gearbox repair, rebuild-kit identification, and replacement around the Geareducer model and complete fan-drive system.
  • Foote-Jones Gearbox & Speed Reducer Planning — Identify a Foote-Jones gearbox or speed reducer and organize the records needed for parts, repair, or replacement evaluation.
  • Philadelphia Gear Repair & Rebuild Planning — Plan repair of Philadelphia Gear equipment using serial identification, original records, inspection evidence, and a clear replacement comparison.
  • Horsburgh & Scott Gearbox Repair Planning — Prepare an H&S gear or gearbox evaluation with equipment records, a defined rebuild scope, and clear requirements for redesign or field work.

Other gearbox makes and legacy identification

For Ingersoll Rand, GE, Santasalo, David Brown, Hansen, Browning, Winsmith, Nuttall, Link-Belt, Philadelphia Gear, Cleveland Gear, Ohio Gear, Horsburgh & Scott, Lohmann & Stolterfoht, Jahnel-Kestermann, Rademakers, Reggiana Riduttori, Brevini, or other equipment, begin with the full nameplate and application. This identification list is not a declaration that every model is supported. Confirm the actual manufacturer, model, and proposed service before selecting parts or arranging shipment.

Choose the service topic

For complete overhaul decisions, read gearbox rebuilding. Use planetary gearbox repair for planetary assemblies, custom gear manufacturing for component-production questions, and industrial motor and pump repair for connected equipment. A brand name does not substitute for a defined service scope.

Location and timing

Include the facility city and state, equipment availability, and required return date. Confirm service coverage, stock, receiving arrangements, and any field-work requirements directly. A nationwide inquiry and a local workshop visit require different logistics; neither should be assumed from a general service description.

Manufacturer reference

Example manufacturer identification resource: NORD. This manufacturer resource supports equipment identification and planning; it does not establish Industrial Gearbox Service as an authorized repair center or parts distributor.

Prepare your equipment details

Record the nameplate, application, symptoms, facility location, and required date in the repair brief. The current private draft saves the brief to your device; it does not send a request. Service suitability, parts availability, and delivery arrangements require confirmation.
