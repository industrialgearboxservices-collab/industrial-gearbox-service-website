Direct answer: Brevini covers more than one reducer design, including industrial planetary gearboxes and helical or bevel-helical units. Use the full series and model code to identify the gearbox before discussing repair, parts or replacement.

Start with the series name and complete type code

Brevini S-Series is a planetary gearbox family described by Dana for heavy-duty industrial uses. Dana materials also distinguish other Brevini lines and applications. Because similar-looking planetary units may have different gear ratios, shaft options, seals, cooling, and input arrangements, record the exact identifier and application instead of matching by housing alone.

Inspection questions specific to this configuration

For planetary units, document the carrier/output arrangement, low-speed shaft, any auxiliary cooling or forced-lubrication package, and the reducer's mounting orientation. In corrosive or wet settings, capture the seal and housing condition. These details help distinguish the configuration; they do not establish the internal failure cause.

Questions about Brevini equipment

How do I identify a Brevini gearbox?

Photograph its complete type plate and record the series, size, ratio, order or serial number, input/output arrangement, and the machine it drives.

Is every Brevini gearbox a planetary reducer?

No. Dana’s Brevini material includes planetary, helical, and bevel-helical families. Confirm the series from the type plate and product documentation.

What should I record on a Brevini S-Series unit?

Capture the S-Series code, ratio, output shaft, motor/input arrangement, mounting, and any auxiliary cooling or lubrication equipment.

Can a Brevini gearbox be replaced by another unit with the same ratio?

Not from ratio alone. Compare output geometry, torque duty, mounting, input connection, environmental requirements, and accessories.

Are Brevini parts available for my model?

Availability varies by series, size, configuration, and part number. Verify current sourcing for the identified unit; this guide does not confirm stock.
