Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a Sumitomo Cyclo gearbox?

Photograph and transcribe the complete nameplate model number, ratio, serial or order number, mounting, and shaft arrangement. Include separate motor and brake plate details when present.

Is Sumitomo Cyclo the same as PARAMAX?

No. Cyclo and PARAMAX are separate product families. Identify the exact series and model code before using a manual or discussing components.

What is different about a Cyclo reducer?

Sumitomo describes the Cyclo 6000 as using a cycloidal disc mechanism. Refer to the exact product catalog because some low-ratio models use helical and planetary gearing.

Can I replace a Cyclo reducer with one that has the same ratio?

Ratio alone is not enough. Confirm the model, capacity, mounting, shaft arrangement, motor interface, duty, and required accessories against the application.

What information should I send for a Cyclo repair inquiry?

Send readable nameplate and overall-unit photos, model and ratio, application, symptoms, operating conditions, lubrication history, and timing.
