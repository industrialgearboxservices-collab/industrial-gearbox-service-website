Flender Gearbox Repair Planning: Start with the Nameplate

Use the Flender rating plate, operating history, and inspection findings to prepare a clearly scoped gearbox repair inquiry.

A Flender gearbox repair request should connect the current symptoms with the equipment’s operating history. Include the drive identification, application, available maintenance records, and any recent changes in temperature, noise, vibration, or load.

Capture the Flender rating-plate data

Flender’s assembly and operating instructions identify the rating plate as a source for the unit’s serial number, type and size, rated power or torque, input and output speeds, and oil type, viscosity, and quantity. Photograph the complete plate clearly and transcribe the values without dropping suffixes. If the plate is damaged or unreadable, mark the uncertain fields instead of guessing.

Flender notes that the intended operating limits depend on the rating-plate data and the unit-specific contractual documentation. Use the matching manual, data sheet, and drawings when available; do not apply values from a similar-looking gearbox to this unit. Flender A5070 assembly and operating instructions.

Use inspection as a decision point

Flender describes its own repair process in stages: receipt and initial checks, disassembly and analysis, repair, and final inspection. Use those stages as questions for a proposed scope; they do not establish that another workshop has Flender authorization or offers identical facilities.

Ask for a condition report before choosing the scope

Agree on how findings will be recorded and when authorization is required. Photographs, component identification, and measured observations help distinguish a localized repair from a wider overhaul. An initial visual check cannot settle every question about internal gear, bearing, shaft, or housing condition.

Carry the operating context into the quotation

  • Identify whether the unit stopped abruptly or developed a gradual change.
  • Supply available lubrication, inspection, and condition-monitoring records.
  • Describe cooling and lubrication equipment included in the shipment.
  • State the production requirement and planned outage dates.

Define completion before work starts

Request the proposed final checks, the documentation to be returned, and any installation or commissioning requirements. Ask which checks are performed unloaded and which require a separately arranged load test. Repair approval should address the work, parts, exclusions, and acceptance criteria together.

Prepare your equipment details

Use the repair brief to organize the nameplate, application, symptoms, facility location, and timing. It downloads to your device; it does not send a request. Confirm parts, service scope, and receiving arrangements for the exact unit before shipping.
