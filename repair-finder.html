IDENTIFY BY GEARBOX DESIGN

Start with gearbox type

Gearbox type describes the gear arrangement or drive design; the application describes what the unit drives. A nameplate, drawing, or clear equipment photo helps distinguish the unit. Use the type-specific guide where one is available.

Planetary gearbox repair

Open the current planetary gearbox repair resource.

Other or unknown gearbox type

Use the general industrial gearbox repair resource and include a nameplate image or drawing if available.

Common design terms include helical, bevel, worm, planetary, and parallel-shaft. A label alone may not identify the exact model or internal configuration; confirm the unit from its nameplate and equipment records.
