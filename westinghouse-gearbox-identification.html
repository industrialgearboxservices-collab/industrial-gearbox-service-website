Westinghouse Gearbox Identification & Legacy Repair Planning

Westinghouse gearbox is a legacy identification term with more than one historical configuration. Preserve original plate and drawing data, and determine the exact unit before evaluating repair or replacement.

Nuttall Gear documents a Westinghouse reducer service line and says it retains original Westinghouse standards for authorized work. This page is an independent identification aid; Industrial Gearbox Service is not presented as Westinghouse- or Nuttall-authorized.

Treat Westinghouse as a legacy equipment identity

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Original nameplate and drawingLegacy equipment may use historic catalog numbers or drawings that no longer resemble a current product code.Capture every plate, stamped serial/order number, gear arrangement, ratio, speed, and associated drawing.
Drive applicationWestinghouse naming has spanned different industrial and power-generation equipment contexts.Record the machine, driven load, motor or turbine input, mounting, output coupling, and operating history.
Service pathNuttall publishes a specific Westinghouse reducer service reference; this does not establish another shop's authorization.Ask the equipment owner to confirm the intended provider, required standards, and whether original drawings or parts are required.

Build a traceable legacy-equipment record

Keep nameplate photos, archived drawings, historical repairs, replaced part numbers, lubrication details, and dimensional measurements together. Note whether the unit has been modified since original installation.

Plan an equipment-specific inquiry

Prepare the gearbox nameplate, full-unit photos, machine or application details, operating symptoms, load and speed history, lubricant records, and available drawings. The repair brief helps organize those details for a review.

Westinghouse gearbox questions

Is there a current Westinghouse gearbox product range?

The reference used here is a legacy service listing. Confirm the specific equipment identity and current documentation rather than assuming a current product family.

Who does Nuttall identify for Westinghouse reducer service?

Nuttall states that it is the authorized company for rebuilding and manufacturing replacement parts to original Westinghouse standards. This site does not claim that authorization.

What details help identify an old Westinghouse gearbox?

Send original plate and drawing photos, serial/order details, application, ratio and shaft data, motor/turbine input, and all available service history.
