Siemens SIMOGEAR Gearbox Identification & Repair Planning

SIMOGEAR identification starts with the complete geared-motor type designation and nameplate. Record the gearbox and motor details separately so the correct configuration and documentation can be matched.

Siemens operating instructions cover SIMOGEAR geared motors and associated components, including handling, installation, operation, troubleshooting, and disassembly. The current instruction set identifies gearbox families E, D, Z, FD, FZ, B, K, C, and S. A family code alone does not identify the size, ratio, mounting, motor, or options of a particular unit.

Separate gearbox symptoms from motor symptoms

For a geared motor, document the gearbox and motor observations independently. Note whether noise or heat changes with motor speed, process load, or operating time. Record any visible leakage and the location, but do not infer the failed component from the symptom alone.

Siemens SIMOGEAR questions

Which gearbox families are covered by Siemens SIMOGEAR operating instructions?

The linked Siemens instructions identify E, D, Z, FD, FZ, B, K, C, and S gearbox families. Verify that the manual revision and exact unit designation apply to your equipment.

Is the motor nameplate enough to identify a SIMOGEAR gearbox?

No. Photograph the gearbox identification and motor plate separately. The geared motor combines components with different identification details.

What details are needed to compare repair and replacement?

Record the complete type and serial or order number, ratio, mounting position, output and input interfaces, motor data, connected load, and inspection symptoms. Verify a replacement against the original application.
