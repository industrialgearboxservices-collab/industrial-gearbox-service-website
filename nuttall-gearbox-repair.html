GEARBOX IDENTIFICATION & REPAIR PLANNING

Nuttall Gearbox Repair & Identification

Nuttall Gear builds heavy-duty speed reducers, gear sets, and custom drive assemblies for industrial applications. Identify whether the equipment is a catalog reducer, loose gearing, or an engineered drive package.

Nuttall's official portfolio includes speed reducers, loose gearing and gear sets, engineered solutions, and application-specific complete drive assemblies. The records needed depend on whether the gearbox is standalone or integrated into a larger system.

Separate Nuttall catalog reducers from custom drive packages

Family or configurationIdentification contextUseful records
Speed reducerUse the nameplate and applicable catalog to identify the reducer family, ratio, arrangement, and size.Provide plate photos, input/output speeds, ratio, shaft details, mounting position, and load history.
Loose gearing or gear setA gear-set inquiry is different from a complete gearbox overhaul because the surrounding casing, shafts, and bearings may be supplied by the machine builder.Send gear markings, tooth counts if known, dimensions, material/drawing records, and photographs of the mating components.
Custom or packaged driveNuttall describes complete engineered packages; identify the entire assembly and its connected components.Include general arrangement drawings, motor/coupling details, foundation interfaces, and the machine duty.

Document the application and operating cycle

For heavy industrial drives, record startup frequency, load variation, driven equipment, direction, duty cycle, and the sequence of recent symptoms. Include alignment or foundation changes when known.

Prepare the gearbox nameplate, full-unit photos, machine or application details, operating symptoms, load and speed history, lubricant records, and available drawings. The repair brief helps organize those details for a review.

Nuttall Gear gearbox questions

Does a Nuttall gear set identify the complete gearbox?

Not necessarily. Nuttall's product range includes both gear sets and complete reducers; identify which components belong to the supplied assembly.

What information should be sent for a custom Nuttall drive?

Send the unit plate, assembly photos, machine and motor details, drawings, duty cycle, and any prior service or operating records.

Is Nuttall the same as Delroyd?

They are related brands within Regal Rexnord, but their product portfolios and gear-unit identification paths differ. Confirm the nameplate before selecting documentation.
