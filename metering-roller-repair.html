Metering Roller Repair

A metering roll helps control the amount of liquid or coating transferred in a roll-coating arrangement. The result depends on roll configuration, liquid properties, speeds, and the required coat weight or film thickness.

Prepare a repair brief ↗

What should I document before requesting roller repair?

  • Identify the applicator and metering rolls as a pair and record each roll’s position.
  • Provide coating chemistry, viscosity or other plant-record properties, line speed, and target coat result.
  • Document streaking, variation, buildup, or surface marks and where they appear.

Before shipment, confirm the agreed service scope, schedule, and receiving arrangements.

Technical context

Metering roller applications describes metering-roll uses; it does not define a universal gap, finish, or coating setting.

How is a metering roller different from an applicator roller?

Roll functions depend on the coating system. In some arrangements, an applicator transfers coating while a separate roll, rod, or blade meters the amount. Other systems use different pairings. Identify each roll by its position in the actual machine instead of assuming that “metering” and “applicator” name the same component.

Valmet’s roll-coating overview describes one paper-coating arrangement; it should not be generalized to metal coil coating or paint lines.

  • Map the coating, applicator, metering, and backing components from the machine drawing.
  • Record substrate, coating product, roll surface specification, line speed, and available coat-weight readings.
  • Describe streaks or bands by their location and repeatability.

A coat-weight change does not prove that the metering roll is damaged. The roll sequence and recorded process conditions provide context for a review.

Metering Roller Repair FAQs

What information matters when a coating line has uneven metering?

Record the roll pair, coating material, operating speed, target coating result, and location of the variation. Include process measurements and the equipment specification so the roll can be reviewed alongside the liquid and setup.

Can I choose a metering-roll gap from a general chart?

No universal gap applies across all coatings and machines. Use the equipment maker’s specifications and the plant’s process requirements; include those records when requesting a scope review.
