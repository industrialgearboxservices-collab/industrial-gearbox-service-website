Eagle Iron Works Gear Reducer Identification & Repair Planning

An Eagle Iron Works machine may include an EIW gear reducer, but confirm the reducer’s own nameplate before assuming its maker or configuration. This guide focuses on identifying the reducer within a washer or sand-processing application.

Manufacturer names and product references on this page are for equipment identification. They do not imply manufacturer affiliation, authorization, or guaranteed parts availability. Confirm service suitability and scope for the exact unit before shipment or work authorization.

Identify the machine and the reducer separately

Start with the washer or equipment model and serial number, then photograph the reducer plate separately. Record the reducer maker, model, serial or order number, ratio, shaft arrangement, and any visible cast or stamped marks. A machine brand is not necessarily the reducer brand.

Why EIW reducer identity matters

Eagle Iron Works has published information about its classic gear reducers and a manufacturer-run exchange program. That program is an EIW offering; this page does not claim that Industrial Gearbox Service provides the same exchange inventory or terms. Verify who owns the unit, the exact reducer model, and the current parts or exchange path directly with the appropriate provider.

For a screw washer, include screw diameter and configuration, material being processed, operating hours, starts and stops, load changes, and any unusual sound, temperature, vibration, or leakage. Note whether the issue is in the reducer, shafting, screw, bearings, or support structure—or whether the source is not yet known.

Information for a repair or replacement review

  • Machine and reducer nameplates, plus overall and connection photos.
  • Ratio, input speed, output speed if known, rotation, mounting, and motor data.
  • Oil type and service records, prior reducer work, and process history.
  • Whether a compatible exchange/rebuilt unit or a new reducer is being considered.

Manufacturer source

Eagle Iron Works’ gear reducer exchange information describes its own program. Confirm current program details and unit eligibility with the manufacturer.

Eagle Iron Works Gear Reducer Identification & questions

Does every Eagle Iron Works washer use an Eagle Iron Works reducer?

Do not assume that from the machine name alone. Check the reducer’s own nameplate, markings, and equipment records to confirm its maker and model.

Does Industrial Gearbox Service offer the Eagle Iron Works exchange program?

The exchange program cited here is an Eagle Iron Works program. This page does not represent that Industrial Gearbox Service offers EIW exchange inventory or program terms.

What should I send for an EIW reducer review?

Provide the machine and reducer nameplates, overall and connection photos, ratio and speed data if available, reducer and oil service history, process duty, and a description of the observed symptoms.
