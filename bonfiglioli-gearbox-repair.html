Start with the Bonfiglioli series

Bonfiglioli’s industrial range includes right-angle A and VF/W families, in-line C units, parallel-shaft F and S products, and the heavy-duty 300 series. The exact product family matters: a compact gearmotor and a large planetary unit have different identification and service questions.

Use symptoms to guide inspection, not to guess the failed part

Noise, heat, leakage, vibration, or loss of output can have more than one cause. Record when the symptom occurs, recent load or speed changes, lubrication history, and any measured temperature or vibration. Inspection findings and measurements are needed before assigning damage to a gear, bearing, seal, shaft, or housing.

Compare repair with replacement on the same basis

Compare the proposed repair scope, identified parts, inspection records, testing, transport, installation work, and schedule assumptions with a replacement quote. Bonfiglioli publishes product catalogs and manuals; use the documents matching the exact product code rather than transferring values from another series.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a Bonfiglioli gearbox?

Record the full type code, series, ratio, mounting position, and serial or order number from the nameplate. Send a clear photo and separate photos of the complete drive and its motor plate.

Which Bonfiglioli gearbox series do I have?

The type code and product documentation identify the family. Bonfiglioli’s industrial portfolio includes A, C, F, S, VF/W, and 300 series products; do not infer a series from appearance alone.

Are Bonfiglioli gearbox parts available?

Availability depends on the exact series, size, configuration, and part number. Confirm current OEM or alternate sourcing after the unit is identified; this guide does not confirm stock.

Can a Bonfiglioli gearbox be repaired instead of replaced?

That decision depends on measured condition, parts, repair scope, and the required duty. Compare an inspection-based repair proposal with the complete installed replacement cost.

What should I send with a Bonfiglioli repair inquiry?

Send nameplate and overall-unit photos, the complete type code, application, symptoms, operating conditions, maintenance history, and required timing.
