SEW-EURODRIVE Repair & Parts Planning

Prepare a SEW-EURODRIVE gearbox or geared-motor repair request using serial-number identification and a clear mechanical and electrical scope.

SEW-EURODRIVE repair services can involve different parts of a drive system. Before requesting a quotation, identify whether the concern is the gear unit, motor, brake, inverter, or their interaction. A complete geared-motor assembly needs a broader intake record than a detached reducer.

Start with serial-number identification

SEW-EURODRIVE provides spare-parts lookup through its online support using the serial number on the component’s nameplate. Keep separate identification photographs for each supplied component. Include existing documentation and describe any previous approved modifications.

Describe the fault without assuming its cause

Record whether the drive fails to start, stops during operation, leaks, becomes noisy, or produces an unexpected speed. Note any controller fault indications exactly as displayed. Mechanical symptoms and electrical faults should be assessed by appropriately qualified personnel within a defined scope; a gearbox rebuild does not automatically resolve a controls problem.

Choose what travels with the unit

  • List the gear unit, motor, brake, cables, sensors, and loose accessories included.
  • Confirm whether the evaluator needs the assembled drive or individual components.
  • Retain identification and photographs of any items left at the facility.
  • Agree on handling and receiving instructions before dispatch.

Review the proposed repair and return records

A useful proposal explains component replacements, inspections, and what will be tested as an assembly. For replacement options, confirm interfaces and configuration against the actual application. The same general product description does not establish interchangeability.

Manufacturer reference

SEW-EURODRIVE spare-parts service. This manufacturer resource supports equipment identification and planning; it does not establish Industrial Gearbox Service as an authorized repair center or parts distributor.

Prepare your equipment details

Record the nameplate, application, symptoms, facility location, and required date in the repair brief. The current private draft saves the brief to your device; it does not send a request. Service suitability, parts availability, and delivery arrangements require confirmation.
