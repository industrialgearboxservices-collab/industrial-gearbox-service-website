Product family and documentation

Official Cleveland Gear literature lists M Series modular reducers, WG reducers, E-Line, fan-cooled, heavy-duty, shaft-mount, and custom drives.

Keep Cleveland's series-specific ratings separate

Cleveland's M Series catalog identifies a modular reducer family with different single- and double-reduction configurations. Its literature separately lists WG, E-Line, fan-cooled, shaft-mount, and custom products. Before comparing repair options, match the installed series and model to the catalog, then capture center distance, ratio, motor adapter, and shaft orientation where documented. A ratio or housing resemblance alone does not establish interchange.

Use symptoms and records to guide inspection

Record the symptom, when it occurs, recent load or speed changes, maintenance and lubricant history, and any measured vibration or temperature. Noise, heat, leakage, or loss of output can have multiple causes; this guide does not diagnose an individual unit or infer a failed part from symptoms alone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a Cleveland Gear reducer?

Record the full series and model designation from the nameplate, plus ratio, mounting, input/output arrangement, and any serial or order number. Add clear photos of the whole unit.

Which Cleveland Gear reducer series may be on my equipment?

Cleveland Gear’s literature index includes M Series, WG, E-Line, fan-cooled and heavy-duty reducers, shaft-mount products, and custom drives. Confirm the series from the plate or original documentation.

Can I use an M Series manual for a WG reducer?

Use documentation that matches the identified series. Cleveland publishes different catalogs and service literature for its product families.

Does noise or heat prove the reducer needs a rebuild?

No. Those symptoms can have multiple causes. Record when they occur and any measured temperature, vibration, load, and lubrication information; inspection is needed to define the work.

What should I provide for a Cleveland Gear repair inquiry?

Send nameplate and overall-unit photos, series/model, ratio, application, symptoms, operating conditions, lubricant details, and required timing.
