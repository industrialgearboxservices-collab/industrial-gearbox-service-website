Product family and documentation

RENK’s extruder gearbox reference describes SUPREX twin-screw gear units and lists CAREX as a related product family.

For an extruder drive, capture both gearbox and process conditions

RENK describes SUPREX as a twin-screw extruder gear unit with an integrated planetary superimposition stage for variable-speed operation; its product data gives a distinct range of output torque, ratios, and center distances. For a unit in this family, preserve information on both output shafts, speed-control arrangement, bearings, and lubrication system. RENK's parts page says gear-unit spares are available by request, so record the type plate and order reference when checking that route.

Use symptoms and records to guide inspection

Record the symptom, when it occurs, recent load or speed changes, maintenance and lubricant history, and any measured vibration or temperature. Noise, heat, leakage, or loss of output can have multiple causes; this guide does not diagnose an individual unit or infer a failed part from symptoms alone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a RENK gearbox?

Photograph the full type plate and record the product family, serial/order number, ratio, shaft arrangement, lubrication configuration, and driven application. Include the original drawing if available.

What are RENK SUPREX and CAREX gear units used for?

RENK lists SUPREX and CAREX within its industrial gearboxes for extruder applications. Use the type plate and manufacturer documentation to identify the exact model.

Can I order RENK gearbox parts from the online shop?

RENK’s current shop page says spare parts for gear units are not currently offered in the online shop and may be available upon request from its sales team. Check the current manufacturer channel for the identified unit.

What records help assess a RENK extruder gearbox?

Gather the nameplate, arrangement drawing, lubrication and oil-condition records, operating data, vibration or temperature trends, and a description of the process and symptom timeline.

Should a RENK unit be repaired or replaced?

Compare inspection evidence, engineering and parts availability, application fit, downtime, and the complete installed replacement scope. The decision depends on the exact unit and operating duty.
