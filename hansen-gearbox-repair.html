Product family and documentation

Hansen P4 includes multistage and single-stage configurations and UniMiner; M4 ACC is a separate standardized gear-unit family.

Hansen industrial gearbox product-family reference · Hansen P4 single-stage product reference

Distinguish P4 configuration before using the drawing

Hansen P4 references cover single-stage and multistage units, while the broader Hansen portfolio also includes M4 ACC and UniMiner. The published P4 single-stage reference shows a horizontal parallel-shaft arrangement and separates monoblock standard units from split-plane heavy-duty units. Record the exact family, stage count, orientation, and drawing number; a generic Hansen label is not enough to transfer dimensions or oil-system instructions.

Use symptoms and records to guide inspection

Record the symptom, when it occurs, recent load or speed changes, maintenance and lubricant history, and any measured vibration or temperature. Noise, heat, leakage, or loss of output can have multiple causes; this guide does not diagnose an individual unit or infer a failed part from symptoms alone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a Hansen gearbox?

Record the complete type/model code, family, serial/order number, ratio, mounting orientation, and stage arrangement from the plate. Include overall-unit and lubrication-system photos.

What is the difference between Hansen P4 and M4 ACC?

They are distinct Hansen product families. Sumitomo’s product references list P4 configurations and M4 ACC units separately; confirm the exact type before consulting documentation.

Does Hansen P4 come in single-stage and multistage versions?

Sumitomo publishes Hansen P4 single-stage and multistage product references. Use the plate and arrangement drawing to establish which configuration is installed.

Are Hansen gearbox spare parts available?

Availability depends on the exact family, type, and part number. Confirm current sourcing after equipment identification; this guide does not confirm inventory.

What information is needed to evaluate a Hansen gearbox?

Provide the type plate, certified drawing if available, application, duty and speed, symptoms, oil-system information, service history, and requested timing.
