Tower Nip Roller Repair

What does this roller do in the process?

A tower nip roll pair is used near the top of some blown-film lines to collapse and draw the film tube. The roll pair and film path determine how the material is handled, so identify the line configuration and layers before interpreting a defect. Identify the complete machine and the roll’s position within it; names and requirements vary among manufacturers and applications.

What should I document before requesting roller repair?

  • Record blown-film line make and model, nip roll dimensions, cover materials, and adjustment arrangement.
  • Identify film structure and layer configuration and provide the plant’s operating records.
  • Describe wrinkles, tracking, gauge, or surface issues and mark their location across the collapsed web.

What details help scope tower nip roller repair?

Include the line and roller manufacturer, model or part number, face and journal dimensions, surface or covering details, operating material, and symptom history. Add drawings, photos, and maintenance records where available. Share your required date and facility location; confirm scope, schedule, and receiving arrangements before shipment.

Technical context

Tower nip roll application describes top and bottom nipping rolls in blown-film extrusion; it does not establish universal settings or repair specifications.

Tower Nip Roller Repair FAQs

What should I provide when a blown-film tower nip creates wrinkles?

Send the line model, nip roll pair details, cover information, film structure, line speed and relevant settings from plant records, plus photos showing where the wrinkle forms. The observation should be considered with the film and line setup.

Are tower nip roll covers interchangeable?

Do not assume that they are. Cover requirements depend on the equipment and process. Provide the OEM specification and the existing roll information when requesting a scope review.
