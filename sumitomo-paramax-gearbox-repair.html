Identify the PARAMAX family and configuration

Sumitomo lists PARAMAX 9000 reducers alongside SFC reducers for cooling fans, SEB reducers for extruders, and SCC reducers for cranes. The 9000 nomenclature encodes details such as mounting, housing, size, shaft position, and number of gear stages. Preserve the full code and suffixes when requesting an evaluation.

What to record before teardown

Note whether the unit is horizontal, vertical, or upright; record the direction of rotation, ratio, lubrication arrangement, and recent operating changes. For a crane, fan, or extruder drive, include the driven equipment and the conditions when the symptom appears. Do not assume a related PARAMAX model shares dimensions or parts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a Sumitomo PARAMAX gearbox?

Photograph the complete nameplate and transcribe the full model/type code, size, ratio, mounting, shaft arrangement, and serial or order number. Include the application and connected equipment.

What is the difference between PARAMAX 9000, SFC, SEB, and SCC?

They are different reducer families; Sumitomo identifies SFC for cooling-fan service, SEB for extruders, and SCC for cranes, alongside the PARAMAX 9000 line. Use the full unit code to confirm the exact configuration.

Can PARAMAX reducer parts be sourced?

Sourcing depends on the exact series, size, and component identification. Confirm OEM and any alternate sourcing options for the unit before authorizing work.

What causes a PARAMAX gearbox to overheat?

Possible causes cannot be determined from temperature alone. Record load, speed, ambient conditions, oil type and level, cooling arrangement, and when temperature rises; inspection and operating data are needed to diagnose the cause.

What information helps quote a PARAMAX gearbox repair?

Provide full plate data, photos, duty/application, operating symptoms, lubrication and maintenance records, recent changes, and the required return-to-service date.
