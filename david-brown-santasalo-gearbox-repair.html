Build a failure history before selecting repair scope

Record what changed before the event, when the symptom began, and whether the unit continued to operate. Note oil condition, leakage location, measured vibration or temperature, and connected-system changes. Distinguish confirmed observations from suspected causes. Frequently asked questions

Is David Brown Santasalo the same as David Brown and Santasalo?

David Brown Santasalo’s company history says David Brown and Santasalo joined forces in 2016. Older equipment may still carry its original David Brown or Santasalo identification.

How do I identify an older David Brown gearbox?

Photograph the original nameplate and record the complete model/type code, serial number, ratio, speeds, mounting, and any drawing or contract number. Do not infer the model from appearance alone.

Can a Santasalo gearbox be repaired if its model is obsolete?

Obsolete status alone does not determine repairability. Identification, inspection, available drawings or samples, part condition, and the required duty inform the decision.

Are David Brown gearbox parts interchangeable with Santasalo parts?

Do not assume interchangeability based on the related company names. Verify exact model, dimensions, revision, and part references against equipment documentation.

What should I send for a David Brown Santasalo repair review?

Send the plate details, overall gearbox and installation photos, application, symptoms, operating history, existing drawings or reports, and target timing.
