Marley Gearbox & Geareducer Repair

Plan Marley cooling-tower gearbox repair, rebuild-kit identification, and replacement around the Geareducer model and complete fan-drive system.

Marley gearbox, Marley gear box, and Marley Geareducer searches often describe a cooling-tower drive inquiry. Record the actual Geareducer identification and tower details before requesting repair, gears, or a replacement unit.

Connect gearbox condition to the cooling-tower drive

Describe the fan-drive arrangement and available service history. Identify whether the investigation includes the coupling, drive shaft, mounting, and connected equipment. A gearbox-only quotation should not be mistaken for an evaluation of the entire tower or fan system.

Rebuild, repair, or replace?

SPX Cooling Technologies describes Marley diagnostics, repair, model-specific rebuild kits, and replacement options. These are manufacturer options to investigate for the identified unit; they are not interchangeable descriptions of every repair. Ask which path suits the observed damage and required outage schedule.

Prepare the model-specific request

  • Supply the Geareducer plate and available cooling-tower identification.
  • Describe the fault, operating conditions, and maintenance history.
  • Identify whether you need components, an evaluated rebuild, or a complete replacement.
  • Include site access and lifting constraints when discussing removal and return.

Coordinate the return to operation

Confirm the installation, alignment, lubrication, and operating checks specified for the equipment. Assign responsibility for the wider fan-drive inspection and keep the repair records with the tower documentation. Obtain any warranty or replacement terms from the actual provider. This page does not promise OEM authorization, stock, lifting services, or a fixed completion time.

For the broader project, see the cooling-tower gearbox repair guide.

Manufacturer reference

SPX Cooling Technologies: Marley diagnostics and repair. This manufacturer resource supports equipment identification and planning; it does not establish Industrial Gearbox Service as an authorized repair center or parts distributor.

Prepare your equipment details
